Privacy Policy

Beam Reflect  ·  Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beam Reflect ("the App," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you download and play the App. Beam Reflect is developed and published by an independent developer. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

Beam Reflect's core gameplay does not require an account, and does not collect your name, email, or any personal contact information directly. The information described below is collected primarily through third-party services we use to run ads, process purchases, and understand crashes/usage.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically, via third-party services

  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data — collected by Google AdMob when banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads are shown, to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics and diagnostic data — collected by Firebase Analytics, including device model, operating system version, app version, general location (country/region level), in-game events (e.g. level start/complete, hint usage), session length, and crash reports.
  • Purchase information — when you make an in-app purchase, Google Play Billing processes the transaction and shares with us confirmation of the purchase and which product was bought. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Google Play.

1.2 Information stored locally on your device

Your gameplay progress — unlocked levels, star ratings, coins, shards, unlocked cosmetics, and settings — is saved locally on your device using standard app storage. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party unless a future update adds optional cloud save, in which case this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App's gameplay and features
  • To display advertisements and measure their performance
  • To process and validate in-app purchases
  • To diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance issues
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns (e.g. which levels players find difficult), so we can rebalance content

3. Third-Party Services

Beam Reflect uses the following third-party services, each of which may collect and process data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Google's Ads Privacy & Terms
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchase processing. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — usage analytics and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy & Security

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Advertising Choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising identifier, through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Or visit Google Ad Settings while signed in to your Google account

Opting out does not stop ads from appearing, but reduces how personalized they are.

5. Children's Privacy

Beam Reflect is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain analytics and purchase data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. We rely on the security practices of our third-party service providers (Google) for data in transit and at rest within their systems.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your data, or to object to certain processing (for example, under the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA). Since most data described here is collected and processed by Google's services (AdMob, Play Billing, Firebase) on our behalf, you can also exercise many of these rights directly through your Google Account privacy controls. For anything specific to Beam Reflect, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
